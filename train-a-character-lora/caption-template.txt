CHRTOK, {view}, {pose}, {expression}, {setting}, {lighting}

view:       front view | three-quarter view | side view | back view | close-up
pose:       standing, arms at sides | arms crossed | walking | sitting on a bed | mid-jump
expression: blank stare | wide grin | sad | surprised
setting:    plain light-blue background | basement with CRT wall | city rooftop at night
lighting:   flat even light | red practical light | moonlight

Examples (one .txt per image, same file name as the image):

  gboy_02.png -> gboy_02.txt

Rule of thumb (general LoRA practice, not from Replicate's docs): caption what changes from image to image. Leave out what never changes
(red horned hood, yellow tee, green cargo shorts, purple sneakers). The trigger
word has to soak those up, so naming them in every caption weakens it.
